AI Background Remover
Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026 · Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what the AI Background Remover app (“the app”, “we”) does with your images and data. In short: your images are processed on your device and are never uploaded by us. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below.

1. Image processing

Background removal runs locally on your device using an AI model. The images you choose are not uploaded to our servers or to any other server by the app. We do not have servers that receive your images.

2. AI models and downloads

The IS-Net General model is built into the app. An optional, more accurate model (BiRefNet Lite, about 200 MB) is downloaded from Hugging Face only if you choose to. Downloaded files are stored in the app’s private storage and checked against a published checksum before use. When you download a model, the hosting server can see standard connection information such as your IP address (see Hugging Face’s privacy policy).

3. Information we collect

The app has no account system and we do not collect, store or receive your personal information on our own servers. Settings (such as whether to use NNAPI or keep history) and your usage counter stay on your device.

4. Ads and third-party services

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads (an interstitial ad after saving an image and rewarded ads that you can choose to watch to continue using the tool). The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google, automatically:

  • IP address, which can help estimate the general location of the device;
  • user product interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app and SDK performance data;
  • device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The advertising ID is optional: you can reset or delete it in your Android settings. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where it is required, the app asks for your consent before ads are requested, using Google’s User Messaging Platform; you can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy options when that option is shown. See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Images and history stored on your device

  • Images you pick are copied to the app’s private storage so they can be processed.
  • If “Save Processing History” is on, the app keeps, privately on your device, the original image, the AI mask and a thumbnail for up to 30 recent results.
  • PNG files you save are written to Pictures/AI Background Remover. Other apps that have access to your photos can see them, and we do not control them.
  • When you tap Share, the PNG is handed to the app you choose.

6. Internet usage

The app uses the internet to download the optional model, to download a model again if its file is missing or fails verification, and to load ads. Your images are never sent over the internet by the app.

7. Data deletion and your choices

  • In the app, Settings → Clear History deletes all stored history, and History → Delete removes a single item.
  • Settings → Manage Models lets you delete downloaded models.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stores privately on your device, including models and history. PNG files saved to Pictures must be deleted from your gallery or files app.
  • Data collected by Google through ads is handled by Google; you can reset your advertising ID or manage ad settings in your Android and Google account settings.

8. Security

Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Model downloads use HTTPS and are verified with a checksum. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
