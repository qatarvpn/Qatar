Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Learn how our app handles information.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Welcome to E2ray VPN. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our mobile application.

E2ray VPN does not require an account or registration. We do not operate our own system to collect personal information directly from users. However, certain third-party services integrated into the app may process technical information as described below.

01 Information We Collect

E2ray VPN does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal details to create an account. No account registration or login is required.

We do not directly collect or maintain a personal user profile through the app. However, third-party SDKs and services used by the application may automatically process certain information, depending on their configuration and your device settings.

  • Technical information about the device and app.
  • Advertising identifiers and advertising interaction data.
  • App diagnostics, crash reports, and performance information.
  • Information needed to deliver and maintain integrated services.

The exact information processed depends on the services enabled in the version of the app you use.

02 Location Permission

E2ray VPN may request access to your device's location to support the interactive map feature.

  • Location access is intended to help display your position on the map.
  • Location permission is not required for account registration because the app has no account system.
  • You can manage or revoke location permission through your Android device settings.

We do not operate our own service to collect or store your location. Nevertheless, map providers or related services may process location information if the map feature connects to them. Please review the applicable provider's privacy policy.

03 VPN Connections and Traffic

E2ray VPN provides VPN connectivity using supported connection protocols and available servers.

VPN connections necessarily involve communication between your device and the selected VPN server. The operation of the VPN service may therefore involve network information such as IP addresses, connection status, and traffic routing data.

We do not claim that all VPN traffic or connection metadata is automatically anonymous or that every server follows the same logging practices. Actual data handling may depend on the VPN server provider, server configuration, and connection protocol.

Do not assume that your internet service provider, destination websites, or VPN server operators cannot observe any information about your connection.

04 Google AdMob Advertising

E2ray VPN uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and its partners may collect or process certain information to serve ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and provide advertising-related services.

This information may include, depending on configuration:

  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Device and app information.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Ad impressions, interactions, and diagnostic information.

Advertising practices may vary depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and the ad configuration.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and the Google AdMob Help Center .

05 Firebase Services

E2ray VPN uses Google Firebase services to support application functionality. Depending on the Firebase products enabled, these services may process technical information such as app interactions, device information, performance data, or crash reports.

Firebase data processing depends on the specific services integrated and their configuration. We do not use Firebase as an account registration system for E2ray VPN.

For more information, please review Firebase Privacy and Security and Google's Privacy Policy .

06 Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information collected directly by us. We do not maintain a user account database for E2ray VPN.

Third-party service providers, including Google AdMob and Firebase, may process information under their own policies to provide the services integrated into the app.

Information may also be processed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service.

07 Data Security and Retention

We aim to use appropriate measures to protect the application and its services. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

E2ray VPN does not maintain a personal account database. Any information processed by third-party providers is subject to their applicable retention and deletion policies, as well as the configuration of the services used.

08 Children's Privacy

E2ray VPN is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The app does not require users to create accounts or submit personal details.

Third-party advertising and analytics services may have their own rules and protections for children. Their configuration should comply with applicable laws and Google Play policies.

09 Your Choices and Permissions

You can control certain aspects of your experience by:

  • Managing location permission in your Android settings.
  • Managing advertising personalization through Google's available settings.
  • Uninstalling the application to stop its future use on your device.
  • Reviewing the privacy policies of integrated third-party providers.

Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete information that may already have been processed by third-party providers. Such information is handled according to their policies.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality, integrated services, or applicable legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

We encourage users to review this page periodically.

11 Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of E2ray VPN, please contact the app developer using the contact information provided on the app's Google Play Store listing.
