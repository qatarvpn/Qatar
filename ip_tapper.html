Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how IP TAPPER ("the App") handles information when you use our VPN application.

IP TAPPER is designed to provide a simple VPN experience. The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use its core VPN functionality.

We do not directly collect or store personal information such as your name, phone number, address, or account password through the App.

The VPN connection may technically process network information that is necessary to establish and maintain the connection. We do not use this information to build a personal profile of you.

Advertising

Permissions
