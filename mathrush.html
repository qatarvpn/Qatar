Privacy Policy for Math Rush

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Math Rush ("the App") handles information when you use our mobile application.

By using Math Rush, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Math Rush does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, address, or phone number.

However, the App uses third-party services that may collect certain information automatically.

These services include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)

The information collected may include device information, advertising identifiers, app usage information, diagnostic information, and other technical data depending on your device settings and the services used.

Math Rush uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob may collect and use information such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Approximate location information
  • Information about interactions with advertisements
  • App usage and diagnostic information

This information may be used to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertising.

Google may use cookies, mobile identifiers, or similar technologies to provide advertising services.

For more information about how Google handles data, please visit:

Google Privacy Policy

Math Rush uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App and to improve its performance and user experience.

Firebase Analytics may collect information such as:

  • App usage and interaction data
  • Device information
  • Approximate location
  • App instance information
  • Crash and diagnostic information
  • General technical information

We use this information to understand how the App is used, identify problems, and improve Math Rush.

For more information, please visit:

Firebase Privacy Information

4. Firebase Cloud Messaging

Math Rush uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send notifications to users.

FCM may process a device or app instance identifier and other technical information necessary to deliver notifications.

Notifications may include game-related updates, reminders, announcements, or other information related to Math Rush.

You can disable notifications at any time through your Android device's notification settings.

Math Rush relies on third-party services provided by Google. These services may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies.

The relevant third-party services include:

  • Google AdMob

We recommend reviewing Google's privacy policy for additional information about how these services process data.

Math Rush is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

8. Data Retention

Math Rush itself does not maintain a personal user account or database containing information directly provided by users.
