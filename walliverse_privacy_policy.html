01 Overview

WALLIVERSE is a mobile app for discovering, viewing, downloading, and setting wallpapers on your device. You do not need to create an account or provide a name or password to use its core features.

This policy applies to your use of the app. Data processing may vary depending on the app version, enabled advertising features, your device settings, and applicable requirements.

02 Information and Data

The following types of information may be processed:

  • Advertising and device data: The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as IP address, advertising or device identifiers, interactions with the app or ads, and diagnostic or performance information for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention.
  • Wallpaper requests: When your device requests a wallpaper from an external service, that service may receive technical connection information needed to deliver the request, such as your IP address.
  • Information stored on your device: Favorites, preferences, or files you choose to download may be stored locally, depending on the features available in your version of the app.

We do not require an account and do not ask you to provide personal details such as your name or phone number to use the app's core features. This does not mean that third-party services integrated into the app collect no technical information.

03 Advertising and Google AdMob

WALLIVERSE uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google, to display ads. Google and its partners may process device and advertising information to deliver and measure ads and to detect abuse or fraud. Depending on your settings, consent, and applicable law, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

Google processes data under its own policies and terms. For more information, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures
  • Google Ads Settings

The information processed may vary by SDK version, app configuration, and user location. This policy should match the app's actual AdMob configuration and the Data safety information submitted in Google Play Console.

04 Wallpaper Sources and External Links

The app retrieves wallpaper information and images from reliable third-party sources selected to provide wallpaper content. When your device loads an image, it connects to the service hosting that image. The service may process connection information according to its own privacy practices.

We do not control the privacy practices, availability, or content of external services. Please review their policies if you need more information about how they handle data.

05 Favorites, Downloads, and Wallpaper Settings

If you use favorites, download images, or set a wallpaper, the app may store files or preferences on your device and use the Android features needed to complete your request. Downloaded files are used to provide the function you selected.

The app does not intentionally send these locally stored files to the developer. Storage details may vary depending on the app version and Android version.

06 Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information that we directly collect. Technical information may be processed or shared by service providers needed to display ads, deliver wallpapers, or protect their services, including Google AdMob and providers hosting wallpaper content, according to their roles and policies. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect rights and safety.

07 Security and Data Retention

We use HTTPS connections where available when communicating with external services and aim to request only permissions needed for app functionality. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Local preferences and downloaded files may remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Data processed by Google or other external services is subject to their own retention and deletion practices.

08 Children's Privacy

WALLIVERSE is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If the app is directed to children or a mixed audience, the developer must configure advertising, consent, and audience settings in accordance with Google Play and AdMob requirements and applicable laws before publishing.

09 Your Choices and Privacy Controls

  • You can manage your advertising ID or available ad-personalization options through Android and Google settings on your device.
  • If the app displays an advertising consent message, you can use the choices provided there to manage your preferences.
  • You can delete downloaded images or clear the app's data in your device settings. This may also remove locally stored favorites and preferences.
  • You can stop using the app or uninstall it at any time.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app, its services, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date. Please review this policy periodically.
