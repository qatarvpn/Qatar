Qatar VPN - Help & Support

Getting Started

Step 1: Open the Qatar VPN application on your device.
Step 2: Select your preferred server from the list by tapping on the "Select Server" button.
Step 3: Tap the power button in the center of the screen to connect to the selected server.
Step 4: Wait for the connection to establish. Once connected, the power button will indicate an active connection.

Key Features

🔌 One-Tap Connection

Connect to your selected VPN server with a single tap on the power button.

🌐 Server Selection

Choose from multiple VPN servers to find the best connection for your needs.

⏱️ Connection Timer

Track your VPN session duration with the built-in timer display.

📶 Connection Status

Clear visual indicators show your current connection status at a glance.

🔒 Secure Protocols

Supports multiple VPN protocols including OpenVPN and WireGuard for secure connections.

⚡ Fast Server Switching

Easily switch between different VPN servers as needed.

Troubleshooting

Connection Issues?
  • Check your internet connection
  • Try switching to a different server
  • Restart the application
  • Make sure you have the latest version installed
