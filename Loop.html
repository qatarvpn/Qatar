Privacy Policy

At Loop Connect, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information gathered while you enjoy our puzzle experience. This Privacy Policy details how we handle data collected through our mobile game.

1. Overview & Commitment

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Loop Connect. By installing and playing Loop Connect, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

2. Information We Collect

We collect limited technical and gameplay information to ensure stability, deliver features, and monetize the game:

Google Play Games Profile

Your Google Play Games Player ID, display name, and avatar are used to manage Leaderboards, achievements, and cloud save state.

Advertising Identifiers

Google Advertising ID (GAID) is collected by Google AdMob to deliver targeted or non-targeted in-game advertisements.

Analytics & Gameplay Stats

Level completion times, daily puzzle attempts, and interaction logs via Firebase Analytics to optimize puzzle difficulty.

Technical & Crash Data

Device type, operating system version, and stack traces via Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose and fix app crashes instantly.

3. Integrated Third-Party Services

Loop Connect utilizes reputable third-party SDKs provided by Google. These services operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob Used for displaying banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads.
    Privacy Terms
  • Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics & Cloud Messaging) Used for crash reports, gameplay telemetry, and push notifications for daily puzzles.
    Firebase Privacy
  • Google Play Games Services Powers global leaderboards, achievement tracking, and sign-in functionality.
    Google Policy

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Game Optimization: To fix bugs, prevent unexpected crashes, and maintain overall game stability.
  • Competitive Features: To rank your top scores accurately on global Leaderboards.
  • Notifications: To inform you about new daily puzzle challenges (you can opt-out via device settings).
  • Monetization: To serve ads via AdMob, enabling Loop Connect to remain 100% free to play.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Loop Connect is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 depending on regional laws). If you believe a child has submitted personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

6. Your Rights & Ad Opt-Out Options

You have full control over your device settings:

Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or limit your Advertising ID directly in your Android phone settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

Data Deletion Request: You can request the removal of any Leaderboard or user data associated with your profile by emailing us.
